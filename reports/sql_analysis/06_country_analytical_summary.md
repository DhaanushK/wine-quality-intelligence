# Country-level Analytical Summary

## Overview

The country-level analysis consolidates critic ratings and listed-price statistics for 17 countries with at least 100 priced review records.

## Key Findings

1. Austria recorded the highest average critic rating of 89.34 and the highest percentage of 90+ reviews at 47.65%.
2. Portugal demonstrated a combination of below-global-average listed prices and above-global-average critic ratings.
3. France recorded an average listed price of $44.91, a median of $25 and a price standard deviation of $69.34, indicating considerable price dispersion.
4. The United States contributed the largest number of priced review records, with 40,351 observations.
5. Romania recorded the lowest average listed price at $16.29 but also the lowest average critic rating at 85.25.

## Analytical Metrics

The consolidated analysis includes:

- Number of priced review records
- Average listed price
- Median listed price
- Average critic rating
- Percentage of reviews rated 90 or above
- Price standard deviation
- Price advantage relative to the global average
- Rating advantage relative to the global average

## Methodology

Only records with non-null, positive listed prices and valid critic ratings were included.

Countries were included when they had at least 100 priced review records.

The global benchmarks were calculated from the individual priced review records.

## Interpretation

The results demonstrate differences in country-level price and critic-rating distributions. Countries with lower average prices do not necessarily have lower critic ratings, and countries with higher average prices do not necessarily have the highest proportion of highly rated reviews.

The findings describe the dataset's review records and should not be interpreted as comprehensive measures of national wine quality, sales or consumer purchasing behaviour.
